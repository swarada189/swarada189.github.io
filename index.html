02 Work

Case studies

Client details are kept confidential. These describe outcomes, not internals.

  • UK fintech · Founding AI engineer Grounded financial-guidance assistant Zero to production in 4 months, then +30% factual accuracy. Read the case study

    Problem

    People wanted financial guidance they could rely on. A chatbot that guesses was not an option.

    Approach

    • Every answer grounded in ~350 expert-written documents.
    • Strict in-scope answering. It declines what it shouldn’t answer.
    • Test-driven evaluation, with ~50 cases per metric.
    • Emotionally aware conversation design for sensitive money topics.

    Result

    Live in four months from a standing start. Factual accuracy then rose 30% through evaluation-driven iteration.

    Stack

    • Python
    • RAG
    • LLM evaluation
    • Guardrails
    • Conversation design
  • Insurance · Telematics Crash detection for usage-based insurance +15% crash-detection accuracy across 500,000+ journeys. Read the case study

    Problem

    A usage-based insurance product depended on spotting real crashes in large volumes of telematics data.

    Approach

    • Scalable data and training pipelines on Ray.
    • MLOps with MLflow and DVC, so experiments and models are tracked and reproducible.

    Result

    Crash-detection accuracy up 15%, measured across more than 500,000 telematics journeys.

    Stack

    • Python
    • Ray
    • MLflow
    • DVC
    • MLOps
  • Industrial · IoT Industrial AI: forecasting, knowledge graphs and vision ~14% lower client operating costs. Read the case study

    Problem

    Industrial clients needed to cut operating costs and get more out of their machine and operational data.

    Approach

    • Idling forecasts built from IoT data.
    • A Neo4j knowledge graph to make operational knowledge queryable.
    • Computer-vision inspection models on Azure ML.

    Result

    The IoT forecasting work cut client operating costs by around 14%.

    Stack

    • Python
    • Forecasting
    • Neo4j
    • Computer vision
    • Azure ML
